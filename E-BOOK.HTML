The Story

Peter Pan is a boy who lives in Neverland, a magical place where children can have adventures, explore new places and never have to grow up.


One night, Peter Pan meets Wendy Darling and takes her and her brothers to Neverland. Together, they experience adventures, meet magical characters and face Captain Hook.

Characters

Peter Pan

A brave and adventurous boy who never wants to grow up.

Wendy Darling

A curious girl who discovers the magical world of Neverland.

Tinker Bell

A magical fairy and one of Peter Pan's closest friends.

Captain Hook

A pirate captain who is Peter Pan's main enemy.

Neverland

Neverland is a mysterious island full of pirates, fairies, adventures and magical places.


It represents imagination, freedom and the desire to keep childhood alive.

How I Created This Website

I created this website using HTML and CSS. I used HTML to organize the information and CSS to design the appearance of the page.

1. HTML

HTML creates the structure of the website, such as headings, paragraphs, sections, buttons and links.

2. CSS

CSS controls the design. I used it to add colors, backgrounds, spacing, sizes and different visual effects.

3. Sections

I divided the website into different sections to organize the information about Peter Pan and Neverland.

4. Navigation

The navigation menu uses links that take the user directly to different sections of the website.
